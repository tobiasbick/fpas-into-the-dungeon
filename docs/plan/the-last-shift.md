# The Last Shift

The shift bell rings beneath the ember forest after forty years of silence.
Three knocks come back through the north grate. The middle one comes twice.
Mara, the last surface keeper, drops what she is holding when she hears it.

## What happened below

During the old flood, Ada signed the closure order from inside the works.
Closing the gate saved the people on both sides. The city marked everyone
below as lost and struck the families from the rolls. Those families built
homes on the far bank. Their children have grown up with the shift bell as
silent as a gravestone.

Now the main dam seat opens a little wider with each return stroke. Edda, a
mechanic in the lower borough, has sounded Ada's old signal. She wants
someone above to answer before a second closure decides her neighbours' fate.
There is time to explore. The failing seat puts a human cost on the choice
without counting down while a player is reading or retracing a route.

| Senn | Reed Bench | Keeps the pilot bell in tune and knows what it takes to summon both crews. |

| Ada | Ada's Window | Survived the old closure. Still has the mitten that matches Mara's. |

Edda and Ada move to the High Landing with the evacuated families. Iven
stays with his boat when the water goes. His answers change with the lake
and the player's final choice.

## The ten districts

Each district contains twenty individually described places. Rooms have stable
IDs, English and German display names, and passages that can be followed in
both directions. The map includes loops and connections between districts.

| District | Landmarks and connections |
| --- | --- |
| First Shift | The pump room, brass key and north grate lead to the grotto and Mara. The muster gate gives access to the archive and the rope market. |
| Ledger Ward | The roll vault holds the two muster names. Archive records, pension claims and deliveries contradict the official death count. A lift links the archive to the kilns. |
| Weeping Locks | The trial weir can lower or refill the lake. The towpath reaches the ferry, the ladder reaches the railway and the relief flume leads to the springs. |
| Keelwater | Iven connects the ferry steps to the far pier at high water. The far pier climbs to the borough. The kiln and signal works provide a dry way around. |
| Cinder Kilns | Pipe moulds, firing tiles and Nell's pressure sleeve explain the old bypass. The mineral lift reaches the salt works during a drawdown. |
| Salt Railway | The head platform stays dry. Low water exposes the track, the shunter and the trim siding. A seep leads out to the springs. |
| Listening Works | The pilot bell summons the crews. The return valve takes readings from the railway and springs. The signal bridge leads to the borough. |
| Lantern Borough | The player can meet the people behind the archive names, find Ada, and muster the families on high ground. The borough steps rise to the dam. |
| Three Springs | The survey table records the pressure for the return conduit. The hinge trail reaches the dam from the other side. |
| Last Seat Dam | Inspection galleries expose the damage and the outlets. The master seat locks a single final choice. |

## Decisions and consequences

To call both crews, learn the cadence from Mara, find the paired names on
the shift roll and use the pilot bell. Speak to Edda to understand the
emergency. From there the player can pursue three endings.

| Choice at the master seat | Preparation | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draw down | Hear Edda and summon the crews. | The cliff spillway saves the lower houses. The lake falls permanently, the ferry grounds and the salt railway stays accessible. |
| Seal | Speak to Iven as well. Muster the families on the High Landing. | The ferry keeps its lake. The lower homes flood after evacuation, their doors close and the residents stay on high ground. |
| Balance | Read the trim scale at low water and the spring survey. Calibrate the return valve. | The return shares the load. Lake and homes survive, and both banks owe a keeper to every future shift. |

The final pin drops once. The player can revisit the world afterwards and hear
what the decision meant to the people who live there. Returning Ada's message
to Mara is a separate choice with its own outcome.
